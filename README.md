## 🎮 About the Game

Released on August 27, 2026, Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is a single-player action-adventure game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Focus Entertainment. Set 15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem, it follows Sophia across the mysterious Minotaur's Island through combat, stealth, exploration, environmental puzzles, and narrative-driven challenges.

It supports the game's sword-focused combat, stealth encounters, exploration, collectible Resonance Points, blades, charms, and temporary combat supplies without changing the core adventure structure.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Sophia's health from being depleted during exploration and combat.
- **Unlimited Resonance Points** — Adds or preserves Resonance Points used to unlock skills across Sophia's mastery branches.
- **Max Skill Progression** — Enables rapid access to available skill upgrades without requiring extended collectible gathering.
- **Stealth Assist** — Reduces enemy awareness during stealth sections and makes infiltration easier.
- **No Fall Damage** — Protects Sophia from damage caused by falls during traversal and exploration.
- **Freeze Mission Timer** — Pauses active timed objectives while leaving normal movement and interaction available.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **God Mode** — Provides complete protection from enemy attacks and environmental damage.
- **Infinite Stamina** — Allows repeated dodges, parries, movement actions, and other stamina-dependent combat maneuvers.
- **Unlimited Blades** — Keeps blade-based combat supplies available during encounters.
- **Unlimited Throwables** — Maintains available knives, bottles, and other temporary combat tools when supported by the current encounter.
- **Easy Parry Timing** — Extends the effective timing window for parries in the game's melee combat system.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Causes regular enemies to fall after a single successful offensive hit.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer's health, stamina, and fall protection options to make traversal across the island more forgiving. Infinite stamina is useful when repeated dodges or sustained movement are required, while No Fall Damage helps during platforming and environmental navigation.

### Tools & Inventory

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy focuses on blades, charms, Resonance Points, and encounter-based tools rather than a traditional crafting system. Use progression options to accelerate skill unlocks and supply options to preserve temporary knives, bottles, and other combat resources during active encounters.

### World & Environment

Sophia explores the Minotaur's Island, solves environmental puzzles, follows hidden paths, and searches for collectibles and ancient secrets. Stealth Assist, Freeze Mission Timer, and No Fall Damage can simplify optional exploration without skipping the game's story objectives.

### Combat & Weapons

The game emphasizes direct sword combat supported by dodges, parries, powerful strikes, and situational throwables. Infinite Health and God Mode provide protection, Easy Parry Timing makes defensive play more accessible, and One-Hit Defeat shortens regular battles.
